# Customer response requirements

Every drafted reply must contain all six elements below, in this order. A reply
missing any element is incomplete.

1. **Acknowledgement** — thank the customer and confirm the ticket reference.
2. **Restatement** — one or two sentences restating the problem in your own
   words, specific enough that the customer can tell you understood it.
3. **Severity and what it means** — state the assigned severity and, in plain
   language, what that means for how it will be handled.
4. **Next step and owner** — the concrete next action and which team takes it
   (Support, Platform Engineering, Billing, Security, or Product).
5. **Timeline** — the first-response commitment from the rubric's SLA for the
   assigned severity. Do not promise a fix date.
6. **Contact route** — how the customer reaches us with more information.

## Constraints

- 120–200 words of body text. Plain English, no marketing language. A subject
  line and the sign-off do not count towards the word budget.
- Do not promise anything the rubric does not commit to: no fix dates, no
  refunds, no root cause you have not been told.
- Do not ask the customer for information already present in the ticket.
- Sign off as "Northwind Cloud Support".
- If the ticket needs information you do not have, ask for exactly that, once.
